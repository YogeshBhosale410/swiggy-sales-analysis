# 🍽️ Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze food-order sales performance across different states, cities, food types, and time periods.

The dashboard provides a clear view of **revenue, orders, customer ratings, food-type performance, geographical sales distribution, and sales trends** to help identify business performance and opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Food delivery businesses generate large amounts of transactional data across restaurants, cities, states, food categories, orders, and customer ratings.

The objective of this project is to transform raw food-order data into an **interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard** that helps answer key business questions such as:

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders were received?
- What is the average order value?
- How satisfied are customers?
- Which food type contributes the most to sales?
- Which states and cities generate the highest revenue?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- Which quarters and months perform better?
- What are the weekly and daily sales trends?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Total Sales
Displays the overall revenue generated from food orders.

### 2. Average Rating
Shows the average customer rating across restaurants and food orders.

### 3. Average Order Value
Measures the average revenue generated per order.

### 4. Ratings Count
Shows the total number of customer ratings/reviews.

### 5. Total Orders
Displays the total number of food orders received.

### 6. Sales by Food Type
Compares revenue contribution between:

- 🥗 Vegetarian
- 🍗 Non-Vegetarian

### 7. Sales by State
Visualizes state-wise revenue distribution using a map visualization.

### 8. Quarterly Performance
Analyzes performance across:

- Q1
- Q2
- Q3
- Q4

using Sales, Ratings, and Orders.

### 9. Top 5 Cities by Sales
Identifies the cities contributing the highest revenue.

### 10. Monthly Sales Trend
Shows how sales change from month to month.

### 11. Weekly Sales Trend
Analyzes weekly fluctuations in revenue to identify high-performing and low-performing periods.

### 12. Daily Sales Trend
Shows sales variation across different days of the week.
